House Hunting Around KU Made Honest

Three straightforward steps to secure clean accommodation before the semester kicks off.

01

Verify True Total Cost

Check the exact monthly rent, security deposit, garbage collection, water supply source, and prepaid KPLC token setup before booking travel.

02

Know the Gate Walking Time

Find out the exact minutes to KU Nyayo Gate, KM Footbridge, or Ruiru matatu stages so you aren't stuck commuting late after evening classes.

03

Inspect Keys Before Paying

Always meet the caretaker at the property gate, confirm water pressure in taps, inspect locks, and always obtain an official receipt for any deposit.
